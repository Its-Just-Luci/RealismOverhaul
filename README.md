This branch is focused on adding hypersonic mid-to-late-century (60s-80s, maybe later eventually) hypersonic air-breathing or air-augmented engines in support of my Advanced X-Planes RP-1 program. All engines are being researched and documented as thoroughly as possible, though due to the nature of some engines, information may be vague and assumptions might have to be made. Where assumptions are made, I will try and explain them in the config notes to the best of my ability.
